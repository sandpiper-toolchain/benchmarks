How it works

A place to compare, openly

Results-first design

Each benchmark is defined around an output format, not an implementation. Contribute a result file and describe your method; the infrastructure handles the rest.

Three kinds of benchmarks

Validation benchmarks have a known answer and score accuracy. Reference benchmarks compare against expert interpretations. Intercomparisons show methods side by side when no ground truth exists.
